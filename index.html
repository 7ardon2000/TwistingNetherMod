When Azeroth broke

Deathwing's return tore Azeroth apart and weakened the barriers between the world and the Twisting Nether. Unstable portals opened, and humans, orcs, night elves and undead were caught in them. Then the portals collapsed.

The survivors were scattered across distant planets and shattered worlds in the Great Dark Beyond. Rumors speak of a way back to Azeroth. Until someone finds it, the old rivals keep fighting to survive.

How to play

Twisting Nether is a mod for StarCraft II melee games: start a custom game on any melee map with the mod.

Discord YouTube
  1. Custom, then MeleeIn StarCraft II, click Custom, choose Melee and select a map.
  2. Create with ModClick Create with Mod, search for "Twisting Nether" and create the game.
  3. Pick your race and playChoose Human, Orc, Night Elf, Undead or Random in the lobby's race list.
