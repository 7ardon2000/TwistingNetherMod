FAQ

Getting started

What is Twisting Nether?

A free mod for StarCraft II that brings the four Warcraft III races into StarCraft II melee games: Humans, Orcs, Night Elves and Undead, with their Warcraft III units and structures. They play with StarCraft II's economy, speed and controls, and every unit is balanced against a StarCraft II counterpart. Watch the introduction video, or see the features and the races.

How do I play?

Play it on any StarCraft II melee map:

  1. In StarCraft II, click Custom, choose Melee and select a map.
  2. Click Create with Mod and search for Twisting Nether.
  3. Create the game, invite your friends or add computer players, pick your race and start.

I can't find it in Create with Mod

Check the spelling ("Twisting Nether") and that you're on the right region. If it still doesn't show up, tell us on Discord which region you play on.

Does it cost anything?

No. The mod is free, and the free version of StarCraft II can play it. Everything downloads automatically when you join or create a game.

How do I pick my race?

Choose Human, Orc, Night Elf, Undead or Random in the lobby's race list. The lobby also has a Warcraft 3 Race option: leave it on Random to use the race list, or set it to force a race.

The races

Where are the heroes?

There are no heroes, items or creep camps. Each race's altar casts seven spells instead, in three tiers: tier 1 right away, tier 2 once your town hall is upgraded (Keep, Stronghold, Tree of Ages, Halls of the Dead) and tier 3 with the last upgrade. Flame Strike, Starfall, Earthquake, Chain Lightning and the rest are still there, cast from the altar onto the battlefield.

Heroes and creeping set the pace of Warcraft III. Twisting Nether plays at StarCraft II's pace instead: expand, build your economy and win with your army, so the power of the old heroes went into the altars.

Can I play a Warcraft race against Terran, Zerg or Protoss?

Not at the moment. Every player picks one of the four Warcraft races. The units are balanced against StarCraft II counterparts, so each matchup plays like a StarCraft II matchup.

What's different from Warcraft III?

  • Minerals and Vespene gas instead of gold and lumber. Every race builds its own gas building on the geysers.
  • 12 workers and 15 supply at the start, 200 supply maximum, no upkeep.
  • StarCraft II speed, controls and hotkeys.
  • Every unit has a StarCraft II counterpart that sets its cost, supply and strength. See balance.
  • New research and abilities, many of them for getting your army up and down cliffs: Giants throw units, Raiders and Huntresses leap, Ghouls tunnel, Mine Shafts and Flying Machines carry your army. See the features.

Where can I look up a unit?

The database lists every unit and structure with its cost, stats, abilities and research, and the search box at the top finds any unit, ability or feature.

Playing

Can I play against the computer?

Yes. Add Computer players in the lobby and choose their difficulty. They play all four Warcraft races.

Is there a ranked ladder?

No. Twisting Nether is played in custom games: any melee map with Create with Mod.

Is it balanced?

Every unit starts from its StarCraft II counterpart, so the races should feel fair from the first game. Real games always find what the numbers miss: if something feels too strong or too weak, tell us on Discord or with the feedback button, ideally with a replay. Every balance update is listed in the patch notes.

Where do I report a bug or send a replay?

On Discord, or with the feedback button at the top of every page. StarCraft II saves your replays in its Replays folder.

About

Who makes Twisting Nether?

It is a free, unofficial fan-made mod. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Blizzard Entertainment. News and videos are on Discord and YouTube.
