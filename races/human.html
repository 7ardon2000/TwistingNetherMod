Stormwind Expedition

Alliance soldiers caught in a portal near the Dark Portal in the Blasted Lands. Far from Stormwind, they have rebuilt their army from whatever metal and machinery they could find.

Footmen, Riflemen and Knights, Flying Machines that airlift infantry over cliffs, Mine Shafts that tunnel an army onto high ground, and an altar that turns minerals and gas into spells.
