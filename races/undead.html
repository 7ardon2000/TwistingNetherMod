The Untethered

A part of the Scourge caught by a rift in the frozen lands of Northrend. Cut off from the Lich King, it fights on as a leaderless horde.

Ghouls that burrow, Crypt Fiends that tunnel, Frost Wyrms, and an altar fed by sacrificed Acolytes. Sacrificial Nexus teleports an army across the map.
