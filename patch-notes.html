Patch notes

Version 1.2

The first release

The first release of Twisting Nether: the four Warcraft III races, rebuilt for StarCraft II. In-game values below are at Faster speed. Every unit's numbers are in the Database, and the reasoning behind them on the Balance page.

Races

  • Human, Orc, Night Elf and Undead, each with its Warcraft III units and structures, rebuilt for StarCraft II's economy, speed and controls.
  • Choose your race in StarCraft's race list. The Warcraft 3 Race lobby option overrides it; leave it on Random to use the list.
  • No heroes and no items. Each race's altar casts spells instead.
  • Computer opponents: all four races play as computer players at every difficulty, with four personalities: rush, balanced, tech and macro.

Economy

  • Minerals and Vespene gas. Every race builds its own Gas Extractor on the geysers.
  • 12 workers and 15 supply at the start, 200 supply maximum, no upkeep.
  • Every structure matches a StarCraft II counterpart by role: cost, hit points, armor 1 and build time. Farms, Burrows, Ziggurats and Moon Wells all cost 12.5 minerals per supply.
  • Workers carry SC2 mineral chunks and gas canisters, and can repair while carrying.

Human

  • City Gate: Peasants build a gate (needs a Blacksmith) to wall off a ramp or a choke. Shut, it stops everyone. Open, anyone can pass and it can't be attacked.
  • Cargo Straps: Flying Machines carry infantry.
  • Mine Shaft: a tunnel onto any high ground.
  • Knight Charge (Barracks): Knights charge an enemy within 6 at double speed.
  • Sundering Blades (Barracks): Knights deal +6 damage to Armored units.
  • Concussive Shells (Workshop): Mortar shells slow the ground units they hit.
  • Swift Gryphons (Gryphon Aviary): Gryphon Riders and Dragonhawk Riders +25% speed.
  • Tower Range (Blacksmith): Guard, Cannon and Arcane Towers +1 range.
  • The Blacksmith does the Lumber Mill's job.
  • Fragmentation Shards: Mortar shells deal 12 more damage where they land, and 7 and 5 more in the two blast rings.

Orc

  • Mount Mastery: Raiders leap cliffs.
  • Goblin Laboratory: Peons build it to train Goblin Sappers, Zeppelins and Shredders. Kaboom! deals 40 (+35 to Light) to units and 120 to structures, and only goes off at enemies.
  • Bombs Away (Goblin Laboratory): Zeppelins drop their Goblin Sappers from the air. Each one bursts the moment it lands.
  • Zeppelin Engines: Goblin Zeppelins +33% speed. Sapper Speed: Goblin Sappers +50% speed. Shredder Saws: Goblin Shredders mine 50% faster.
  • Berserker Speed (Barracks): Troll Berserkers +25% speed.
  • Swift Wyverns (Beastiary): Wind Riders +25% speed.
  • Demolisher Range (War Mill): Demolishers +2 range.
  • Reinforced Defenses: Burrows and Watch Towers get +2 armor.

Night Elf

  • Strong Roots: uprooted Ancients walk slowly over cliffs.
  • Improved Intelligence: Mountain Giants and Ancient Protectors throw units, up cliffs too.
  • Strong Branches: Ancients carry ten Archers. Improved Stability: the Archers fire from the branches.
  • Ancient Sentinel: a rooted Protector with true sight.
  • Cliff Leap (Hunter's Hall, needs a Tree of Ages): Huntresses leap up and down cliffs.
  • Swift Wings (Ancient of Wind): Hippogryphs, Hippogryph Riders and Chimaeras +25% speed.
  • Rooted Armor (Tree of Ages): rooted Ancients +3 armor.
  • Photosynthesis: with no trees to eat, an uprooted Ancient channels by day to restore 250 life.

Undead

  • Ghoul burrow: Ghouls burrow like Zerglings.
  • Hardened Claws: burrowed Crypt Fiends and Ghouls tunnel, up cliffs too.
  • Sacrificial Nexus: the Halls of the Dead teleport an army.
  • Plague Corpses (Temple of the Damned): Meat Wagons lob a carried corpse that bursts and raises two Skeleton Warriors for 20 seconds.
  • Crypt Fiend Range (Crypt): Crypt Fiends +1 range.
  • Bone Plating (Temple of the Damned): Skeleton Warriors and Skeletal Mages +2 armor.
  • Abomination Speed (Slaughterhouse): Abominations +25% speed.

Active abilities

Like the Stimpack, these last 11 seconds and have a 30 second cooldown, and each has its own look. - Footman, Defend: half damage from ranged attacks, under a pale hex shield. - Grunt, Brute Strength: +50% melee damage, in a fire aura. - Archer and Hippogryph Rider, Improved Bows: +2 range, in a violet light. - Ghoul, Ghoul Frenzy: +50% attack speed and +30% movement speed, in a crackling green shell.

Altars

Each altar casts seven spells in three tiers, paid in its race's own currency: Orc energy that recharges, Human minerals and gas, Night Elf energy fed by Moon Wells, Undead energy from sacrificed Acolytes. The full list is on the Features page. - Altar spell bar: your altar's seven spells sit in a column on the right edge of the screen, tier 1 at the top, framed in pieces of your race's own interface. Cast from anywhere without selecting the altar: click a spell, or press Ctrl+Q, W, E, R, A, F or D, then click the target. The bar shows each spell's cost, cooldown and tier. - Spells kill what they hit: Chain Lightning 80, Death Coil 75, Fan of Knives 50, Impale 50 plus 20 on landing, Thunder Clap 45. - Starting Energy (research at each race's caster building): new casters start with +25 energy.

Balance: StarCraft II counterparts

Every unit has a StarCraft II counterpart that sets its cost, supply and strength, and each race's version of a role plays differently. - Tier 1: the Grunt is a Zealot (2 supply, armor 1), the Footman half a Zealot, the Archer a Marine, the Ghoul two Zerglings. - Siege: between a Siege Tank's tank and siege modes. The Mortar Team is fastest with the longest range, the Glaive Thrower and Meat Wagon sit in the middle, and the Demolisher is the toughest and hardest-hitting. - Heavy melee: the Tauren is the Ultralisk. The Knight is the fastest, the Mountain Giant the toughest wall, and the Abomination a cleaving brawler. - Air: the Flying Machine and Hippogryph answer the Phoenix, the Gargoyle the Viking, and the Dragonhawk Rider the Mutalisk. - Capital ships: the Chimaera hits the ground hard, and the Frost Wyrm hits air and ground. - Armor: units have their counterpart's base armor.

Feel

  • Units turn, accelerate and sidestep like StarCraft II units; armies clump like SC2 armies.
  • Attacks land quickly after the swing, so kiting and stutter-stepping work.
  • No random damage, and no missed shots uphill.
  • A day and night cycle with moonlit nights.
  • Thrown units flip through the air and land with a thud, and burrowing units throw up a trail of dirt.

Interface

  • Targeting circles for spells, range indicators for towers and wards.
  • "Not enough minerals / gas" voice lines for each race.
  • Hotkeys for every unit, building, research and ability, with no two sharing a key on one command card, and the Twisting Nether title for a few seconds when a game starts.
